  • Games are just for fun. Pick'em, predictions, power rankings and fantasy leagues have no entry fees, money or prizes.

  • No warranty. The site is provided as is and may change or go offline at any time.

Community rules

Chat, comments and leagues are for fans to have fun together. Trash talk is welcome. Being a jerk isn't.

  • No spam. No links, ads, self-promotion or posting the same thing over and over. Links are blocked in chat and comments.
  • No harassment. Rag on teams, not people. No threats, hate speech, or targeting someone over who they are.
  • Keep it legal. No illegal content, no sharing other people's private info, and no pirated game streams.
  • Don't impersonate players, staff, or other members.
  • Swearing is fine. We don't filter language. Just keep it about the game.

Breaking the rules can get your posts removed or your account banned. To report a problem, contact the site owner.
