North Huddle privacy policy

North Huddle (north-huddle.pages.dev) is an unofficial fan site. Not affiliated with the Minnesota Vikings or the NFL.

What we collect

If you create an account, we store your email address, the display name and profile photo you choose (or the name and photo from your Google account if you sign in with Google), and what you post: chat messages, reactions, score predictions and fantasy league activity. Browsing without an account collects nothing about you.

How it's used

Only to run the site: signing you in, showing your name and photo next to your posts, and running fantasy leagues and leaderboards. We don't sell or share your information and don't show ads.

Where it's stored

Account data is stored with Supabase. The site is hosted on Cloudflare.

Deleting your data

To delete your account and everything you posted, contact the site owner and it will be removed.
